Day 18 - Off the Rails (warmups-day-18)

Prompt: "Three rails. One very zigzag commute." Layer: rail fence cipher, 3 rails.

Single Solve / Time Trial

Blob is the carrier sentence ("Nice work decoding this warmup! To solve, submit this exact answer to the Game Master: <slug>") transposed with a 3-rail rail fence. Decoding with k=3 recovers it. AUTOSOLVE (rail-fence crib decode) solved both live.

  • single_solve: CORRECT, flag Tokencd9560835728caf286a2b8556e3c8e8c
  • time_trial: CORRECT, flag Token060bbc46a75ecf9873c67623ebc8df31

Mastery

88 sets, each a blob-NN.txt of 18 numbered lines, one fixed codec per line, concatenating to the carrier + slug: 1 base64, 2 rot13, 3 hex, 4 binary, 5 decimal, 6 octal, 7 percent, 8 html entities, 9 JSON \u, 10 base32, 11 base64, 12 reverse, 13 atbash, 14 caesar+7, 15 rot47, 16 Morse spelling hex bytes, 17 Baconian (a..p = hex nibbles), 18 (new) 3-rail rail fence on the final slug tail. Line boundaries shift per set, so the lines are concatenated. First attempt failed adapter validation (line 18 unknown, nothing submitted). Fix: added LINE_DECODERS["rail3"] and appended "rail3" to LADDER_NAMES in challenges/_lib/autosolve_warmups.py; offline check decoded 88/88 dumped sets (solver: scripts/solve_mastery.py, check: scripts/check_adapter.py). Rerun of autosolve submitted via op=practice.

  • mastery: CORRECT, flag Token4193e013ac835ee9808a2408dd77e2fd (example set1 slug: lucky-pillow-copper-56)

Lesson

New mastery line = a new transposition on the slug tail; add the decoder to the ladder (one line), validate offline on the dumped blob.
