Lessons Learned

  • training_rotation_invalid 503 is the timed ranked rotation pool, a separate dual-pool route (run_for_time:true) from regular play. Per the GM openapi/guidance a mode is still completable via regular untimed play / op=practice - do not treat the 503 as "unsolvable", and do not hammer start on a shared timer.
  • op=status is read-only and reports the current result/flag per mode - the cheapest way to learn a mode is already solved (ours showed all three CORRECT once the rotation recovered) and to reconcile a stale local board against GM ground truth.
  • The new layer was exactly one step over Day 07 (nested-ZIP EOCD/central-directory inventory); the banked metadata adapter handled the rest.
