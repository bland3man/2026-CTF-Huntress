Dependency Surface (mobile-android, Day 27)

Prompt: prove which generated dependency flaw is reachable from an exported mobile surface.

Method: the artifact is an Android APK. The framework's mobile solver (autosolve_mobile via agent_solve_cid.py, per-CID working copy in scripts/solve.py) decoded the answers statically from the APK, including a secondary DEX for the dynamic codec. Codecs seen: base64, caesar, hex, reverse, split, xor. The APK was not executed. Single had 1 answer; Time Trial and Mastery each re-derived their answers from that attempt's own bytes (Mastery had 11 answers).

Results (all GM-accepted, solved by Blandman3):

  • Time Trial: 07804559eeeec1976ba264dfd54ec322
