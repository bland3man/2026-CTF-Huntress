Mastery - CORRECT

  • Flag: Token302326d42a9989de1c205c937ff05a79
  • A zip of 73 blob-NN.txt files, each with 15 numbered lines, one fixed encoding per line.
  • Ladder: base64, rot13, hex, binary, decimal, octal, percent, html, json-u, base32, base64url, reverse, atbash, caesar+7, rot47 (new line 15).
  • Example: blob-01 -> tidy-basket-copper-68.
  • First attempt was WRONG. The ladder had grown to 15 lines and the autosolver had no decoder for line 15 in the slug region. The generic fallback then guessed ROT13 for lines 13 and 14 and produced gibberish slugs such as tejo-lumeyn-wopper-68.
  • Lines 13 and 14 are atbash and caesar+7, not ROT13. Line 15 ("@AA6C\eg") is ROT47 of "opper-68".

Fix (challenges/_lib/autosolve_warmups.py)

  • Added rot47 to LINE_DECODERS and appended it to LADDER_NAMES.
  • compute() now saves multi-set mastery bytes to challenges/_pool/_dump_warmups/ (timed artifacts are not cached).
  • compute() now raises instead of submitting a multi-set decode that does not contain the known sentence.
  • Verified offline on all 73 real blobs (73/73 real-word slugs) before the one GM submit.
  • Lesson recorded as kind gap.
